# FINBUD Brand Identity

## Brand direction
A modern, trustworthy personal-finance platform. Use the supplied logo artwork as the recognizable brand reference. The overall product UI must use a LIGHT theme.

## Core palette
- Brand green: #10B981
- Deep green: #087F5B
- Dark ink: #1F2937
- Primary text: #111827
- Secondary text: #64748B
- App background: #F7FAF8
- Surface / cards: #FFFFFF
- Subtle border: #E5E7EB
- Soft green surface: #ECFDF5
- Warning: #D97706
- Danger: #DC2626

## Logo usage
- Use `svg/finbud-logo.svg` for scalable web use.
- Use `png/finbud-logo-vector.png` for transparent PNG use.
- The supplied original is retained in `png/finbud-logo-original.png`.
- Use the dark wordmark variant on light backgrounds so the white portions of the supplied artwork do not disappear.
- Do not stretch, skew, rotate, add 3D bevels, or add glow effects.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of the letter F.
- For compact navigation, use `svg/finbud-icon.svg` or `png/finbud-icon-512.png`.

## UI principles
- Light, calm, clean, data-first interface.
- White cards on a very light neutral/green-tinted canvas.
- Green for positive performance, savings, growth and primary actions.
- Red only for negative movement, errors and risk.
- Use restrained shadows, thin borders and consistent rounded corners.
- Maintain accessible contrast and visible focus states.
